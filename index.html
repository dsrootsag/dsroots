Our Story

DS = DeviSara.
Roots = soil, family, legacy.

DS ROOTS AGRO was born from memory — the memory of a mother, a family's hard work, and a deep bond with the land. What began as remembrance is now growing into an integrated agro-farm for the next generation.

“DS” is not a marketing acronym. It is a name. DeviSara. This farm is her memorial, and everything we grow is rooted in her memory.

Our Businesses

Six integrated pillars. One farm.

Flagship · Priority 01

Kalij Farming

DS ROOTS AGRO's primary business is Kalij farming and approved Kalij activities — pursued responsibly, under strict government license and wildlife compliance.

  • Rearing & farm management
  • Breeding & record keeping
  • Health & biosecurity
  • Feed & nutrition management
⚠️ No Kalij activity begins without prior government approval. “Approval First, Operation Second.”
Priority 02

Local / Native Chicken

Local breed conservation, rearing, chick production and meat production — flow: Rearing → Production → QC → Processing → Sales.

Priority 03

Layer & Egg Production

Commercial layer farming with graded, quality-controlled eggs — collection → inspection → grading → storage → packaging → sales.

Priority 04

Timur Cultivation

High-value spice business — from nursery to plantation, harvest, drying, processing, packaging, branding, and market.

Priority 05

Agro-Processing & Value Addition

Cleaning, sorting, grading, drying, processing, packaging, labelling, branding, storage — turning raw produce into market-ready products.

Priority 06

Branding, Marketing & Distribution

Farm → Storage → Processing → Packaging → Distribution → Customer. Channels include wholesale, retail, B2B, B2C, institutional, and contract supply.

"पहिले अनुमति → त्यसपछि सञ्चालन"
